## How Detroit pulled away

The Detroit Lions beat the Arizona Cardinals 45-21 at State Farm Stadium. Detroit was favored by 5.5 points, and the game turned decisively in the second quarter.

Arizona had the early push. Brissett hit Bourne for 22 yards, a 0.095 win-probability swing for the Cardinals. Wilson then threw to Brissett for 7 yards, down to the Detroit 2, and Brissett finished the drive with a 2-yard touchdown pass to Quitoriano. ([Q1 5:10](/games/2026/week-05/#play-484)) Detroit's first quarter was messier: Goff took a 12-yard sack from Baker that cost 0.075 in win probability.

The second quarter flipped it. Goff found LaPorta for 12 yards, and a 15-yard unnecessary roughness penalty on Arizona was tacked on. The play was worth 0.102 in win probability. Jahmyr Gibbs then scored from a yard out. ([Q2 12:25](/games/2026/week-05/#play-1237)) Goff followed with a 38-yard touchdown to Sam LaPorta, the biggest swing of the game at 0.172, or 17.2 percentage points. ([Q2 9:38](/games/2026/week-05/#play-1567)) A 23-yard Goff pass to Gibbs set up the next score, and Goff hit Jameson Williams for an 8-yard touchdown. ([Q2 4:55](/games/2026/week-05/#play-1946))

Detroit kept going after halftime. Goff threw a 39-yard deep touchdown to Williams in the third quarter. ([Q3 9:50](/games/2026/week-05/#play-2833)) Arizona answered when Brissett found Quitoriano from 13 yards. ([Q3 4:27](/games/2026/week-05/#play-3134)) Goff closed the scoring with a 1-yard touchdown run up the middle. ([Q3 1:07](/games/2026/week-05/#play-3374)) Detroit finished 4-for-4 on fourth down.
