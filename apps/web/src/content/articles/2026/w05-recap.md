## How the game was decided

The Detroit Lions beat the Arizona Cardinals 45-21 at State Farm Stadium as 5.5-point favorites. Efficiency and explosive plays settled it. Detroit averaged 0.326 EPA per play to Arizona's 0.022, and its explosive-play rate of 15.4% far outpaced Arizona's 5.7%. Detroit did not turn the ball over. Arizona gave it away once.

The team grades split sharply: 95 for offense, 95 for special teams and 45 for defense, for an overall 72.5. These grades are provisional.

## Turning points

Arizona had the early edge. Jared Goff was sacked by Baker for a loss of 12 yards at the Detroit 24 in the first quarter, a play worth -2.653 EPA and -7.5 points of win probability. Brissett then hit Bourne for 22 yards, a 9.5-point swing toward Arizona. A fourth-down completion to Brissett moved the ball to the Detroit 2, and Brissett scored on a 2-yard pass to Quitoriano. ([Q1 5:10](/games/2026/week-05/#play-484))

The game turned in the second quarter. Goff hit Sam LaPorta for 12 yards, and an unnecessary roughness penalty on Arizona added 15 more. That play was a 10.2-point swing. Jahmyr Gibbs scored from a yard out moments later. ([Q2 12:25](/games/2026/week-05/#play-1237))

The biggest swing of the game followed. Goff found LaPorta for a 38-yard touchdown, worth 17.2 points of win probability and 5.574 EPA. ([Q2 9:38](/games/2026/week-05/#play-1567)) Goff then threw to Gibbs for 23 yards, and Jameson Williams caught an 8-yard touchdown. ([Q2 4:55](/games/2026/week-05/#play-1946))

Detroit extended the lead in the third quarter when Goff's deep right pass to Jameson Williams went for a 39-yard touchdown. ([Q3 9:50](/games/2026/week-05/#play-2833)) Arizona answered when Brissett hit Quitoriano for a 13-yard touchdown. ([Q3 4:27](/games/2026/week-05/#play-3134)) That response moved win probability by only 3.2 points. Goff then scored on a 1-yard run. ([Q3 1:07](/games/2026/week-05/#play-3374))

## Offense and defense against the numbers

Detroit's passing game did the damage: 0.496 EPA per dropback against 0.151 per rush attempt. Arizona managed 0.123 per dropback, and its rushing attack was negative at -0.294 EPA per attempt.

The Lions went 4-for-4 on fourth down, against Arizona's 2-for-3. Third down was Detroit's weak spot: it converted 30.8%, well below Arizona's 44.4%. Arizona's success rate of 44.3% was also close to Detroit's 47.7%, so the Cardinals did move the ball. They lacked the big plays to turn that into points, and the 22-yarder to Bourne was the main exception.

Sacks were even. Detroit's sack rate was 9.1% and Arizona's was 9.4%. Detroit was flagged four times for 31 yards, Arizona five times for 45.

The defense's grade of 45 is the lowest of the three units, even in a 24-point win. Arizona's offense was efficient in spots and scored 21.
