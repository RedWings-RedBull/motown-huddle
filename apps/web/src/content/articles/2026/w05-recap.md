## How the game was won

The Detroit Lions beat the Arizona Cardinals 45-21 at State Farm Stadium, a result well beyond the 5.5-point spread they carried into the game. The offense earned a Den Grade of 95 and special teams also graded 95. The defense came in at 45, which left the team at 72.5 overall.

The per-play numbers show the gap. Detroit averaged 0.326 EPA per play with a 47.7% success rate. Arizona managed 0.022 EPA per play and a 44.3% success rate. The explosive-play split was wider: Detroit hit on 15.4% of its plays, Arizona on 5.7%. Detroit finished with no turnovers, while Arizona's offense gave it away once.

The passing game decided it. Detroit generated 0.496 EPA per dropback and 0.151 EPA per rush attempt. Arizona's passing offense reached only 0.123 EPA per dropback, and its rushing attack came in at -0.294 EPA per attempt.

## Turning points

Detroit's start was uneven. Early in the first quarter, Jared Goff was sacked for a 12-yard loss, a play that cost 7.5 points of win probability (Baker made the sack) ([Q1 13:39](/games/2026/week-05/#play-106)). Arizona then moved the ball. A 22-yard Brissett completion to Bourne added 9.5 points of win probability for the Cardinals. A fourth-down play later in the quarter, a 7-yard completion that put the ball at the Detroit 2, added another 6.3. Brissett then hit Quitoriano from 2 yards out for a touchdown ([Q1 5:10](/games/2026/week-05/#play-484)).

The second quarter changed the game. A 12-yard Goff completion to Sam LaPorta drew an unnecessary roughness flag on Arizona, and the combined play was worth 10.2 points of win probability. Jahmyr Gibbs scored from a yard out soon after ([Q2 12:25](/games/2026/week-05/#play-1237)). The biggest swing of the game came next. Goff found LaPorta for a 38-yard touchdown on a fourth-down play, which moved Detroit's win probability by 17.2 points ([Q2 9:38](/games/2026/week-05/#play-1567)). Goff then hit Gibbs for 23 yards, and Jameson Williams caught an 8-yard touchdown pass to extend the run ([Q2 4:55](/games/2026/week-05/#play-1946)).

Detroit kept going after halftime. Goff's deep right throw to Williams covered 39 yards for a touchdown ([Q3 9:50](/games/2026/week-05/#play-2833)). Arizona answered with a 13-yard Brissett touchdown pass to Quitoriano ([Q3 4:27](/games/2026/week-05/#play-3134)). Goff closed the third-quarter scoring with a 1-yard run up the middle on a fourth-down play ([Q3 1:07](/games/2026/week-05/#play-3374)). That fits Detroit's 4-for-4 mark on fourth downs, against Arizona's 2-for-3.

## Offense and defense against the numbers

Detroit's offense was efficient but not clean. It converted just 30.8% of its third downs, and its sack rate was 9.1%. Four penalties cost 31 yards. The fourth-down success covered for the third-down trouble.

The defense had a more mixed day. Arizona converted 44.4% of its third downs, well ahead of Detroit's 30.8%, which fits the 45 defensive grade. Against that, Arizona's rushing EPA of -0.294 per attempt, its 5.7% explosive rate and its one turnover show where the defense did its work. Arizona's offense was sacked on 9.4% of its dropbacks. The Cardinals also committed five penalties for 45 yards, compared with Detroit's four for 31.
