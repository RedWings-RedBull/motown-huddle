## How the game was won

The Detroit Lions beat the Green Bay Packers 20-17 at Ford Field, and the finish was the only tidy part of it. Jake Bates kicked a 22-yard field goal with one second left in the fourth quarter, a play worth 0.129 in win probability. Detroit was a 3.5-point favorite, but it needed that late drive to get there.

Jameson Williams caught a 16-yard touchdown from Jared Goff in the first quarter ([Q1 7:08](/games/2026/week-07/#play-482)), and Green Bay answered when Love hit Kraft for 14 yards with 1:04 left in the period ([Q1 1:04](/games/2026/week-07/#play-837)). Detroit then fumbled early in the third quarter ([Q3 14:16](/games/2026/week-07/#play-2136)), and Love's 25-yard touchdown pass to Jacobs followed ([Q3 12:22](/games/2026/week-07/#play-2263)). Goff and Williams connected again from 10 yards out later in the third ([Q3 6:40](/games/2026/week-07/#play-2576)).

Detroit's overall team grade was 39.286, a modest number for a win. The defense carried the best mark at 55.357. The offense graded 26.786 and special teams 23.214.
