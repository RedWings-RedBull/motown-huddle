# Data license

The **code** in this repository is MIT licensed (see `LICENSE`).

The **generated data** under `apps/web/src/data/**` and `apps/web/public/data/**` is derived from
sources with their own terms and is published under
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
(CC BY-SA 4.0), because some inputs are ShareAlike.

Attribution for the inputs:

- Play-by-play, player and team statistics, rosters, depth charts, snap counts, injuries, schedules and
  betting lines: [nflverse](https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data) (CC BY 4.0). Schedule and line data
  are maintained by Lee Sharpe.
- Charting data (drops, contested targets, pass-rusher counts, ...): FTN Data via nflverse (CC BY-SA 4.0).
- Advanced statistics (pressures, missed tackles, coverage): Pro-Football-Reference via nflverse.
  Only team-specific derived percentiles are published; no raw league-wide tables are committed.
- Individual Next Gen Stats figures cited in prose: NFL Next Gen Stats via nflverse.
- Contract years: OverTheCap via nflverse.
- Player social handles: seeded from Wikidata (CC0), then hand-verified.
